Sadikoi is the daily question game for your group of friends.

Every day, your group gets a new question, like "Who's most likely to miss their flight?" or "Who flips the table when they lose at Uno?". Everyone votes for the friend who fits it best, and the results show up as bubbles: the more votes you get, the bigger your bubble.

WIN ITEMS AND DEFEND THEM
The most voted friend wins the item of the day: the Dunce Cap, the Leader's Cape, the Traitor's Mask, the Torch of Chaos and more. They keep it until someone takes it from them, and the whole group sees how many days they held on.

HOW IT WORKS
• Create a group and invite your friends with a link
• Pick the themes you like: Party, Travel, Food, Love, School, What if, Spicy and many more
• Choose the time the new question arrives each day
• Vote, see the results and argue about them in the group chat, GIFs included
• Some questions ask you to pick a duo
• Look back at past questions in the calendar

MADE FOR YOUR FRIENDS
• As many groups as you want: high school friends, roommates, family, sports team
• Notifications for new questions, messages and vote reminders, adjustable for each group
• More than 1,300 questions in 25 themes
• In English and French
• No ads

Have fun, and go easy on the friend who keeps winning the Dunce Cap.
